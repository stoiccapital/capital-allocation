Money Game
Every second = one year of your life

How to play

You have $ 100 000. Grow it as much as you can.
1
Buy things that make money

Stocks pay you a tiny bit every year. Houses pay you rent. Businesses give you their profits. Pick a tab and click Buy.

2
Press Play (top right of this box)

Time starts moving. Every second in real life = one year in the game. You can speed it up with 10× or 100×.

3
Your money grows by itself

Earnings pile up in your bank. Use them to buy more things. More things earn more money. This is called compounding, and it's magic.

4
Watch out for crashes

Markets crash every few years. Prices drop. Don't borrow too much money — if you borrow a lot and prices crash, the bank takes your stuff and you can go broke.

5
Spend some on fun stuff

Click Buying stuff at the top to spend money on cars, art, watches, houses. Some lose value (yachts). Some grow in value (Patek watches, Ferraris, Picassos).
